# Lumi Bot — Slash Command Quick Reference

All slash commands. Type `/` in any channel to autocomplete.

---

## 💰 SadGirlCoin (SGC)

You earn SGC automatically by chatting (~1 SGC per 50 characters; images +25, videos +50). Smoke a cigarette for a temporary multiplier.

**`/lumi-bank balance`** — your SGC balance + server top 10
**`/lumi-bank send <user> <amount> [note]`** — send SGC to another member
**`/lumi-bank raffle`** — buy a yearly raffle ticket (50 SGC)

---

## 🎤 Voice Channel Tracker

**`/lumi-vc rank`** — top 20 VC time leaderboard
**`/lumi-vc me`** — your own VC time stats

---

## 🎰 Casino

**`/lumi-pachinko peg:<1-10> bet:<sgc>`** — drop a ball, bet on a peg

**`/lumi-slots start`** / **`/lumi-slots leave`** — open or join a slot machine

**`/lumi-blackjack play bet:<sgc>`** — sit at the multiplayer BJ table
**`/lumi-blackjack bet amount:<n>`** — change your bet for the next hand
**`/lumi-blackjack leave`** — leave the table

**`/lumi-holdem play bet:<ante>`** — sit at the Texas Hold'em table
**`/lumi-holdem bet amount:<n>`** — set your ante for the next hand
**`/lumi-holdem raise amount:<n>`** — raise during your turn
**`/lumi-holdem leave`** — leave the table

**`/lumi-horserace start`** / **`/lumi-horserace leave`** — horse race lobby

---

## 📈 LumiBets — Prediction Markets

**`/lumi-bets list`** — show all open markets with current odds
**`/lumi-bets buy market:<id> option:<name-or-#> amount:<sgc>`** — invest in an outcome

Payouts are automatic when the market resolves.

## 💹 LumiStocks — Stock Exchange

**`/lumi-stocks list`** — all listed tickers with price and 24h change
**`/lumi-stocks portfolio`** — your holdings + unrealized P/L
**`/lumi-stocks buy ticker:<sym> amount:<sgc>`** — buy shares for that SGC amount
**`/lumi-stocks sell ticker:<sym> shares:<n>`** — sell shares (fractional allowed)

## 👘 Touhou Collector & Battler

**`/lumi-touhou menu`** — interactive menu (easiest entry point)
**`/lumi-touhou adopt`** — random Touhou for 25 SGC
**`/lumi-touhou collection [user]`** — view a collection
**`/lumi-touhou info name:<name>`** — full stats + lore
**`/lumi-touhou search query:<q>`** — search the catalogue
**`/lumi-touhou stats`** — server-wide collection stats

Trading & marketplace:
**`/lumi-touhou send name:<name> user:@user`** — gift a Touhou
**`/lumi-touhou trade yours:<a> user:@user theirs:<b>`** — propose a 1-for-1 swap
**`/lumi-touhou sell name:<name> price:<sgc>`** — list on the marketplace
**`/lumi-touhou buy name:<name>`** — buy a listed Touhou
**`/lumi-touhou market`** / **`/lumi-touhou listings [page]`** — browse listings

Battling:
**`/lumi-touhou buy item:Health Potion amount:<n>`** — buy potions (20 SGC each)
**`/lumi-touhou battle name:<name> rarity:<tier>`** — fight an opponent of that rarity (or `gamble` for random)
**`/lumi-touhou heal name:<name> [pay:true]`** — heal a Touhou (auto-heal slow; `pay` rushes for SGC)
**`/lumi-touhou party`** — show your active battle squad

## 🚬 Cigarettes & Smoke Boosts

**`/lumi-cigarette gacha`** — pull a random cigarette (1 SGC)
**`/lumi-cigarette case [user]`** — view a case (each cig has a slot number)
**`/lumi-cigarette leaderboard`** — top smokers
**`/lumi-cigarette smoke slot:<n>`** — 5-min 1.25x–3x earnings boost (rarity = strength)
**`/lumi-cigarette buff`** — your active boost + time remaining
**`/lumi-cigarette trade`** — trade a cig for another, or sell for SGC (recipient must approve)

## 💬 Talking to Lumi (no command)

She reads the channel on her own. To guarantee a reply, **mention her** (`@Lumi`) or **reply** to one of her messages. She has long-term memory — correct her if she gets something wrong.
